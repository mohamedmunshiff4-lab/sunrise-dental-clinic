Help - How to Use the System

A step-by-step guide for new staff members.

1. Log in. Enter your username and password on the login page. If you see "Invalid username or password", check for typing mistakes or ask the administrator.
2. Register a new appointment. Click "Register Appointment" in the menu. Fill in the patient name, address, 10-digit contact number, select the dentist and treatment, and choose a date and time. Time must be a 30-minute slot between 09:00 and 16:30. When you save, the system shows the new appointment number (for example APT-2024-0001). Give this number to the patient.
3. Find an appointment. Click "Search" and type the appointment number to see the full patient and appointment details.
4. Print a bill. Click "Billing", enter the appointment number, and press "Generate Bill". The bill adds the consultation fee (Rs. 1000) to the treatment cost. Tick the loyalty box for regular patients to apply a 10% discount. Use the Print button for the receipt.
5. Reports. Click "Reports" to see the daily schedule, revenue and appointment statistics. Pick a date and use Print for a paper copy.
6. Log out. Always click "Logout" when you finish, so patient data stays protected. Never share your password.
